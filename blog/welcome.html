January 10, 2025

Welcome to my blog! This is where I'll be sharing my thoughts on robotics, AI, and the various projects I'm working on.

This blog will be my space to document these explorations, share insights from my research, and discuss the broader implications of advances in robotics and AI.

Stay tuned for posts about:

  • Technical deep-dives into robotics and AI research
  • Behind-the-scenes looks at my hardware projects
  • Thoughts on the future of embodied AI
  • Lessons learned from building robotic systems

I'm excited to share this journey with you!
